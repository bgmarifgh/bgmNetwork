01 / The Idea

Make unused bandwidth
useful — on your terms.

bgmNetwork is exploring a voluntary network where people may choose to contribute a limited portion of their internet connection and receive rewards when eligible, permitted network activity is used.

These are proposed product principles, not currently available features. A working service depends on technical validation, partner approval and applicable legal and network requirements.

02 / Non-Negotiables

Trust isn't a feature.
It's the foundation.

01

Explicit permission

No sharing without a clear, informed opt-in. Every session starts with the user's deliberate choice.

02

Visible controls

Clear usage information, pause controls and a straightforward way to opt out at any moment.

03

Responsible network use

Any future service must include safeguards against abuse and comply with partner, ISP and local requirements.

04

Honest rewards

No guaranteed income claims. Any payouts would depend on actual eligible demand and published terms.

03 / Proposed Flow

How a consent-first
network could work.

High-level conceptual flow. Subject to technical validation and partner integration.

STEP 01

Install & Opt-in

User downloads the client, reviews terms, and explicitly enables sharing with chosen limits.

STEP 02

Secure Tunnel

Encrypted, auditable connection routes only permitted traffic through approved partners.

STEP 03

Usage Tracking

Transparent metrics show volume, sessions and estimated contribution in real time.

STEP 04

Reward Settlement

Verified eligible usage is settled according to published partner terms — never guaranteed.
