01 / THE IDEA

Make unused bandwidth
useful — on your terms.

bgmNetwork is exploring a voluntary network where people may choose to contribute a limited portion of their internet connection and receive rewards when eligible, permitted network activity is used.

01
＋

Choose to join

People would install the app and actively opt in. Participation would never be enabled silently.

02
⌁

Set your limits

Users would be able to review usage, pause sharing and leave the network whenever they choose.

03
↗

Understand rewards

Any reward model would be explained clearly, based on verified usage and the terms of an approved network partner.

These are proposed product principles, not currently available features. A working service depends on technical validation, partner approval and applicable legal and network requirements.

02 / NON-NEGOTIABLES

Trust isn't a feature.
It's the foundation.

01

Explicit permission

No sharing without a clear, informed opt-in.

02

Visible controls

Clear usage information, pause controls and a straightforward way to opt out.

03

Responsible network use

Any future service must include safeguards against abuse and comply with partner, ISP and local requirements.

04

Honest rewards

No guaranteed income claims. Any payouts would depend on actual eligible demand and published terms.

04 / QUICK ANSWERS

Before you ask.

Is bgmNetwork available to download?

No. bgmNetwork is currently a pre-launch project. There is no public app or active bandwidth-sharing service yet.

Is bgmNetwork partnered with Bright Data or another provider?

No partnership is being claimed at this stage. We are exploring potential SDK and infrastructure partners.

Will users earn a guaranteed amount?

No. Earnings cannot be promised. Any future reward model would depend on partner approval, eligible demand, actual usage and published terms.

Does this website use my internet connection?

No. This is an informational website only. It does not install software, route traffic or share your connection.
